    hello, gardener

    this is a garden that only grows what people draw in it. press the pencil and spray a flower. you don't choose the colour or the brush: the garden picks them for you, a new colour with every stroke.

    nothing here lasts. a planted flower withers, grain by grain, until it's gone. tend it, by opening it and drawing into what's left, and it starts over. or pick it: it goes into the album and stays the way it was.

    the gate is the way in and out. the tree is just somewhere to sit for a bit.
